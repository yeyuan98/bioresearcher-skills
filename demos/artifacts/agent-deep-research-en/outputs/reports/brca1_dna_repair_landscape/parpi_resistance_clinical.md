# PARPi Resistance & Clinical Landscape: BRCA1-Deficient Cancer (TOPIC: brca1_dna_repair_landscape)

Scope: This aspect surveys the 2023–2026 literature on therapeutic exploitation of BRCA1 loss, strictly along the therapy-response/resistance axis: (a) PARP inhibitor (PARPi) sensitivity and resistance mechanisms in BRCA1-mutant tumors (reversion mutations, replication fork protection, shieldin/53BP1-pathway alterations, drug-tolerant/adaptation states); (b) homologous recombination (HR) restoration and its functional assays; (c) combination strategies in BRCA1-deficient settings (ATR/CHK1/WEE1 inhibitors, immunotherapy, chemotherapy partnerships); and (d) recent high-profile trial results and the trial landscape explicitly involving BRCA1-deficient / HR-deficient populations. Purely biochemical mechanism studies without therapy relevance, non-BRCA HR-gene-only studies, and germline-risk/epidemiology-only studies are excluded (owned by parallel aspects).

## Findings

### (a) PARPi sensitivity and resistance mechanisms in BRCA1-mutant tumors

- **Reversion mutations.** Secondary intragenic BRCA1/2 mutations that restore the reading frame and partial protein function remain the prototypical acquired-resistance mechanism to PARPi synthetic lethality in BRCA1/2-mutated breast, ovarian, pancreatic, and prostate cancers [@pmid:42274517]. A complementary real-world observation reframes reversions' clinical importance from the opposite direction: ovarian carcinomas with *homozygous deletions* of HRR genes — by definition not susceptible to secondary reversion mutations — showed the most durable PARPi benefit. Among 5,404 advanced ovarian cancers, 1.5% (82) carried HRR homozygous loss; among all BRCA1/2-altered ovarian cancers 7.5% (63/836) were homozygous losses, and most BRCA1 losses (34/38, 89%) were intragenic. In the maintenance cohort, PARPi use was associated with strongly improved PFS in homozygous-loss tumors (HR 0.06, 95% CI 0.01–0.37); in the monotherapy cohorts, median PFS was 4.9, 10.6, and 25.4 months and median OS 18.5, 22.5, and 51.8 months for no HRR alteration, HRR mutation/rearrangement, and HRR homozygous loss, respectively [@pmid:42133897].
- **Replication fork protection and gap suppression.** Current syntheses reframe PARPi cytotoxicity around single-stranded DNA (ssDNA) gaps and transcription–replication conflicts rather than double-strand breaks alone, positioning restoration of replication-gap suppression as a central resistance route in HR-deficient (including BRCA1-mutant) tumors [@pmid:42590926]. A hierarchical framework organizes DDR-inhibitor resistance as a progression from pathway restoration (e.g., HR restoration) to replication-stress tolerance states [@pmid:42116135] — the adaptation/tolerance phenotype that the persister literature describes (see Evidence Gaps for the BRCA1-specific persister gap).
- **Shieldin/53BP1-pathway alterations.** Loss of 53BP1/shieldin-complex function, which removes the block to end resection in BRCA1-deficient cells and thereby restores HR, is catalogued among the established clinical resistance classes, alongside a collateral vulnerability: DNA ligase III dependence in 53BP1-deficient resistant tumors [@pmid:42590926]. A resistance-pharmacology review formally classifies "53BP1/Shieldin-mediated HR restoration" as one of six mechanistically distinct clinical resistance categories, together with BRCA1/2 reversion mutations, replication fork stabilization, Polθ-mediated TMEJ, ABCB1 efflux, and cGAS–STING-mediated immune evasion [@pmid:42314345].
- **RAD51 axis.** RAD51 hyperactivation is repeatedly listed among established PARPi resistance mechanisms in BRCA1/2-mutant tumors [@pmid:42590926], and a breast-cancer-focused review traces RAD51's dual role as vulnerability and resistance driver [@pmid:42490812].
- **Determinants of residual PARPi sensitivity without BRCA1.** GSK3β-directed chromosomal repair-pathway selection was shown to support BRCA1-independent PARPi sensitivity, indicating that repair-pathway choice — not BRCA1 status alone — governs PARPi response when BRCA1 function is absent or bypassed [@pmid:41243967].

### (b) HR restoration and functional assays

- Clinical HRD testing today relies mainly on DNA-sequencing-based genomic "scar" assays (LOH, mutational signatures), while functional assays remain under validation for routine diagnostics; a 2025 technical-validation review maps the HRD testing landscape and its clinical applications [@pmid:39985088].
- Functional, protein-based readouts are advancing: in an ovarian carcinoma PDX platform with known cisplatin/olaparib response, a low BRCA1-foci score (immunofluorescence on FFPE slices) predicted olaparib and cisplatin response, a high score predicted resistance, and combining BRCA1-foci with RAD51-foci scores improved predictive value over either test alone [@pmid:38989145].
- Phospho-RPA2 was reported to predict response to platinum and PARP inhibitors even in HR-proficient ovarian cancers, extending functional HR-state readouts beyond BRCA-mutant/HRD-scarrable disease [@pmid:40392598].
- Integrated dynamic biomarker frameworks propose combining HRD scar assays, functional RAD51-foci analysis, replication-gap profiling, and ctDNA monitoring for real-time resistance tracking [@pmid:42590926]; a biomarker-guided adaptive-treatment proposal similarly combines longitudinal ctDNA, functional RAD51-foci assays, and AI-driven multi-omics integration [@pmid:42314345]. These functional-assay proposals are being prospectively interrogated in the clinic, e.g., an actively recruiting study of POLQ/shieldin-complex/53BP1 expression as biomarkers of PARPi resistance in germline BRCA1/2-mutated HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer [@nct:NCT05378204].

### (c) Combination strategies in BRCA1-deficient settings

- **ATR inhibition.** The ATR inhibitor AZD6738 (ceralasertib) overcame acquired olaparib resistance in BRCA1-mutant TNBC models (olaparib-resistant HCC1937 and MDA-MB-436 lines): ATR+PARP dual inhibition showed synergistic cytotoxicity, accelerated cell-cycle progression, induced DNA damage and apoptosis, significantly suppressed olaparib-resistant HCC1937 xenograft growth in vivo, and downregulated BRCA2 and RAD51, reversing HR-repair signaling activation [@pmid:42120731]. Reviews concur that ATR-inhibitor combinations show promising clinical activity in PARPi-resistant HR-deficient ovarian cancer [@pmid:42590926] and map each resistance class to matched next-generation DDR strategies [@pmid:42314345].
- **Broader DDR target landscape.** In TNBC — the BRCA1-disease archetype — next-generation DDR targets under mechanistic and early-clinical evaluation include ATR/CHK1, WEE1, DNA-PK, RAD51, POLQ, neddylation-related pathways, and targeted protein degradation, with PARPi/platinum as the validated HRD-exploiting backbone and most newer agents still preclinical or in early trials; BRCA reversion, HR restoration, fork stabilization, and checkpoint adaptation limit durable benefit [@pmid:42451670]. PROTAC-based PARP1 degraders are advanced as a strategy to overcome resistance and induce alternative death pathways [@pmid:42590926].
- **Clinical combination partnerships.** Resistance-class-to-strategy mappings are supported by 2024–2026 trial data including PETRA, EvoPAR-Prostate01/02, STELLA, MEDIOLA, TOPACIO, ATHENA-COMBO, CAPRI, and DUO-O [@pmid:42314345]. Directly in germline BRCA-mutated breast cancer, an actively recruited/ongoing trial tests olaparib combined with cediranib or with AZD6738 (ceralasertib) in advanced/metastatic disease [@nct:NCT04090567].

### (d) Recent trial results and trial landscape in BRCA1/HRD populations

- A phase II trial of niraparib in advanced pancreatic cancer with pathogenic variants in ATM, BRCA1, BRCA2, PALB2, and CHEK2 (Clin Cancer Res, 2026) extends PARPi monotherapy evidence explicitly including BRCA1-mutant disease beyond breast/ovarian cancer [@pmid:41686836].
- Real-world clinico-genomic evidence (Flatiron–Foundation Medicine) demonstrates durable PARPi effectiveness in ovarian cancers with HRR homozygous loss and argues for reporting this alteration class — immune to reversion — on clinical sequencing assays [@pmid:42133897].
- Pan-cancer 2026 syntheses consolidate the clinical PARPi application space (breast, ovarian, prostate, pancreatic) and emerging resistance-countering strategies — ATR/CHK1/WEE1 and PI3K/AKT combinations, immunotherapy, and hormonal partnerships — supported by 2024–2026 trial data including PETRA, EvoPAR-Prostate01/02, STELLA, MEDIOLA, TOPACIO, ATHENA-COMBO, CAPRI, and the practice-changing DUO-O trial [@pmid:42314345].
- The actively recruiting trial landscape directly addressing BRCA1-deficient resistance includes olaparib + ceralasertib/cediranib in germline BRCA-mutated metastatic breast cancer [@nct:NCT04090567] and the POLQ/shieldin/53BP1 resistance-biomarker study in germline BRCA1/2 HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer [@nct:NCT05378204].
